# Brand & marketing

# Our brand

Software is everywhere—but **good software is hard to find**. In game development, teams repeatedly rebuild code, extending timelines and costs. Singularity exists to turn proven code into reusable infrastructure: discoverable, verified, adaptable, and monetizable.

Our mission is to **empower developers**—from creator to entrepreneur—by making code reuse trustworthy and economically rewarding.

# Marketing vision

We will:

- Establish Singularity as the trusted marketplace for indie scripting assets.
- Grow a creator ecosystem by making onboarding easy (repo linking) and verification automatic.
- Use the gaming sandbox to perfect the integration engine, then expand to broader software development.
