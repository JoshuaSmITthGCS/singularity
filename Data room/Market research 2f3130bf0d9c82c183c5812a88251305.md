# Market research

## Market size

- **TAM (General software development):** ~$522B
- **SAM (Game development):** ~$141.5B
- **SOM (Indie scripting beachhead):** ~$1.31B

## Growth trajectory

- **Start in gaming** where cross-engine, multi-language scripting pain is high and adoption cycles are faster.
- **Broaden capability** from scripting assets to larger systems/components and studio pipelines.
- **Expand beyond games** by applying the same “verify + adapt” engine to general software code reuse.

## Competitive landscape

Singularity differentiates by combining:

- **Quality verification** (automated checks) vs. unverified code sharing.
- **Cross-language/engine adaptation** (LLM-assisted) vs. engine-specific marketplaces.
- **Creator monetization + attribution** concepts (UID/smart contracting) vs. one-time asset sales.

## Go-to-market strategy

- **Target early adopters:** indie teams and technical creators in Unity/Unreal ecosystems.
- **Core message:** “From idea to trusted code in four steps.”
- **Initial traction channels:** partnerships/outreach through gaming programs and developer communities.
